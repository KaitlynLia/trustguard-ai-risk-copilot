Financial Transaction Risk Review Policy

1. Suspicious Transaction Activity
Transactions may require review if the amount is unusually high relative to the customer's historical behavior.

2. Rapid Movement of Funds
Multiple large transfers within a short period may indicate elevated risk.

3. Account Age Risk
New accounts with high-value transactions should receive additional scrutiny.

4. Geographic Risk
Transactions involving high-risk jurisdictions may require enhanced due diligence.

5. Inconsistent Customer Profile
Transactions inconsistent with stated income, occupation, or normal activity patterns should be escalated.

6. Review Outcomes
Analysts may approve, reject, or escalate a case depending on risk severity, available evidence, and policy alignment.
