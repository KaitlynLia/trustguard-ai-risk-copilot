E-Commerce Return and Refund Policy

1. Standard Return Window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.

2. High-Value Electronics
Electronics above $500 require additional review before refund approval. Refunds may be escalated if the customer has repeated refund activity.

3. Damaged Item Claims
If a customer claims an item arrived damaged, the customer must provide photo evidence within 7 days of delivery.

4. Frequent Refund Behavior
Accounts with more than 3 refund requests within 60 days should be flagged for manual review.

5. Final Sale Items
Final sale items are not eligible for return unless required by law.

6. Refund Abuse Indicators
Refund requests may be escalated if there are signs of policy mismatch, repeated high-value returns, inconsistent explanations, or missing evidence.
